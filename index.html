origin_story.txt

How Pip arrived

Three scenes. Scroll slowly.

  1. 1

    The nursery went quiet

    One night the Midnight Nursery ran out of dreams. The mobiles stopped turning, and the ceiling stars flickered like they were holding in a yawn. Then a small cloud drifted through the window with a very sleepy passenger. Pip wore a nightcap, a pink bow, and a wave. “Hi,” said Pip. “I think I’m the new night shift.”

  2. 2

    The music box remembered

    Under the crib, Pip found an old music box and wound its golden key. One chime rolled across the floor, and the room remembered how to listen. Every note became a stitch of dream, sewn into pillows and blankets. Pip learned fast: you can’t rush a lullaby, but you can keep it company.

  3. 3

    The first delivery

    By morning the nursery glowed. Pip hung a crescent lantern in the window so no dream would get lost on the way in. The first delivery went perfectly: one cozy dream, one sleeping baby, zero spills. Pip yawned, tucked in the stars, and clocked out until sunset.
